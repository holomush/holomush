<objective>
Close G-06.1-2: the md/lg breakpoints are hand-duplicated across seventeen
authored sites — sixteen `@media` viewport rules plus the Sheet's own
`matchMedia` query string — with nothing keeping them equal.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 asks that the rail's collapse and the admin shell's
collapse fire at the same moment BY CONSTRUCTION. Today they fire together by
coincidence — seventeen independent literals that happen to agree. This plan
replaces the coincidence with one token on the CSS side, one shared constant on
the JS side, and a committed boundary assertion that fails when they diverge.

Output: the JS bridge routed through the shipped `mediaQuery` hook, all sixteen
authored `@media` viewport rules derived from Tailwind's own breakpoint tokens,
a source census that forbids a new literal in EITHER spelling, and a 767/768
(plus 1023/1024) boundary sweep in the E2E spec.

**Scope note — this is the full sweep, chosen by the developer over the
admin-only alternative.** The band is spelled two ways in this tree and both are
equally prevalent: seven files carry the `max-width` complement (`767`/`1023`),
nine rules between them, and six carry the `min-width` form (`768`), seven rules
between them. Converting only one spelling would ship a census that forbids
`max-width: 767px` while tolerating `min-width: 768px` one directory over — an
exemption nobody could justify later. Five of the six added files are Phase 5's
surface and TopBar is the earlier shell epic's; see `<blast_radius>` for what
that costs and how it is covered.
</objective>
