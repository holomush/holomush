### CONFIRMED — the invariant registry is 70% unproven

`docs/architecture/invariants.yaml` holds **361** invariant entries, of which

`pending` is an explicitly tolerated state (`.claude/rules/invariants.md`,

(CRYPTO at 84% pending, ACCESS at 73%) are the least mechanically pinned, and
`INV-TELEMETRY` is 100% pending. Fix shape: the ratchet already exists
(`// Verifies: INV-<SCOPE>-N` + `binding: bound` + `go run ./cmd/inv-render`);
what is missing is throughput, not mechanism.

## Security Considerations

### CONFIRMED — ABAC residue is filed but open

The `abac` label carries a cluster of open, mostly medium findings against
`internal/access/`:

- #4933 — `profilevis` conjunction never binds property rows to the character
  being rendered (`aspect:security`, `deferred-work`).
- #4934 — viewer property twins lack the `parent_type=="character"` guard their
  grid-path sibling carries (`aspect:security`).
- #4935 — nil `CharacterOwnerResolver`/`PlayerRoleLookup` is silent, breaking an
  established WARN precedent. A silent nil in an authorization resolver is the
  classic fail-open shape.
- #4969 / #4970 — an unconditional permit seed with no production consumer, and
  `world.JobCaller` construction sites with no census, so the job instance-fence
  is ungated.
- #4899 — unresolved design question: admin authority is `PlayerHasRole`, i.e.
  **player-wide**, and nobody has decided whether it should be per-character.
  Until decided, every character of an admin player carries admin authority.
- #4950 / #4951 — `reservedActionKeys` is a denylist rather than a supply-side
  allowlist, and there is no host-vouched attribute channel separate from
  `AccessRequest.Attributes`. `.planning/phases/02.2-.../02.2-SECURITY.md:65`
  records T-02.2-16 (caller self-assertion of a host-vouched key) as
  **transferred** to #4951, explicitly not reachable today.

Denylist-over-allowlist (#4950) and caller-supplied attributes reaching the
policy engine (#4951) are the two structural ones — the rest are instance bugs.

### CONFIRMED — best-effort session invalidation on password reset

`internal/auth/reset_service.go:213` notes that making session invalidation
mandatory (returning an error on failure) is only "considered". Today a reset
can succeed while existing sessions survive.

### CONFIRMED — the crypto surface is the least test-pinned

102 of 122 `INV-CRYPTO` entries are `binding: pending` (above). The repo
compensates with a dedicated adversarial `crypto-reviewer` gate
(`CLAUDE.md` → Pre-Push Review Gates), which is a human/agent control, not a
mechanical one. Pair that with #4932 (KEK not in backups) and #4928 (KEK
passphrase not provisioned) and the crypto subsystem's *operational* envelope is
the weakest part of it.

the coverage gate is unachievable. Net: the merge gate is weaker than the
documented policy claims.
