<objective>
Wire the `job:` principal into the PRODUCTION ABAC stack, and make a job's denial say which kind of
principal was denied as well as which kind of resource.

Purpose: plan 01 proved the model against hand-built resolvers. Until `BuildABACStack` registers
the job provider, a job subject resolves to no `job.*` attributes in production and every job seed
silently default-denies — the exact `holomush-g776`/`xxel` bug class the seed-coverage sweep exists
to warn about. D-58's deny code is what lets alerting distinguish "a job was denied on a character"
from "a player was denied on a character" without parsing log attributes.

Output: production provider registration, a single injected `jobs.Registry`, and the composed
`JOB_<ENTITY>_ACCESS_DENIED` code.

(The `<assumption_delta_decision>` block for this phase lives in `02.2-01-PLAN.md`; its consequence
binds here too — every job authorization path in this plan goes through `world.Caller`.)
</objective>
