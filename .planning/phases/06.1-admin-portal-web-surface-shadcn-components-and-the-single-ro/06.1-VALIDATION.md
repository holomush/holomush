### Anti-vacuity findings

This phase had a documented history of false greens, so the guards were checked
against their own falsifiability rather than merely run.

**The prior "census rejects its own repair" defect is genuinely gone.** `48rem`
and `64rem` are now entries in `forbiddenViewportWidths`
(`web_phone_band_breakpoint_census_test.go:108`) and the exemption is
symbol-scoped (`:110-129`), so `DESKTOP_MEDIA_QUERY = '(min-width: 48rem)'`
(`mediaQuery.svelte.ts:31`) satisfies the staleness check at `:469` instead of
tripping the offender list. **The rem/px divergence is closed at the source**:
`rg "min-width: 768px|max-width: 767px" web/src` returns no match.

**The census cannot pass by reaching nothing.** `require.Empty(t, offenders)`
(`:504`) is backed by an occurrence-equality assertion at `:580`
(`ruleCensusOccurrences + allowlistLines == bandRelevant`) and by
`require.Empty(t, unclassified)` (`:430`) under an **inverted, fail-closed**
extension filter.

**The E2E absence-grep is the correct shape.** `admin-portal.spec.ts:755` matches
`/admin|moderation|permission|denied|forbidden|unauthoriz|not allowed|403|401/i`
— case-**insensitive**, applied to whitespace-normalized `.notfound` text, and
preceded by three positive `toBeVisible`/`toContainText` assertions so an empty
locator cannot make it vacuously green. It is further backed by
`new Set(renderings).size === 1` at `:772`.

**The formerly self-echoing assertion is gone.** `EditCharacterSheet.svelte.test.ts:165-179`
has been replaced by `:175-210`, scoped to *internal* consistency and opening
with an explicit anti-vacuity test at `:176` ("declares at least one path").
Cross-language pinning moved to the Go guard, which asserts
`world.MaxNameLength` / `world.MaxDescriptionLength` **by value**
(`admin_characters_web_parity_test.go:300,304`) — the caps are no longer
comment-linked only.
